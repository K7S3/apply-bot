Senior Data Scientist — Acme Analytics (fictional company)
New York, NY · Hybrid

About the role:
We're hiring a Senior Data Scientist to join our risk modeling team. You'll design
and analyze experiments, build ML models for fraud detection, and partner with
product and engineering to ship data products.

Requirements:
- 4+ years of experience as a data scientist or related role
- Strong SQL skills, including window functions over large event tables
- Deep experience with A/B testing and experimental design
- Hands-on machine learning: XGBoost, scikit-learn, model evaluation
- Python and pandas for analysis; statistics fundamentals
- Excellent communication with non-technical stakeholders

Nice to have:
- Causal inference (difference-in-differences, propensity matching)
- Airflow or dbt experience
- Fintech background

Compensation:
The salary range for this role is $150,000 - $185,000 per year, plus annual bonus
and equity. Benefits include health, dental, 401(k) match.
