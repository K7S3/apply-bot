# Behavioral Interview Questions

A starter set of classic behavioral questions to prepare for a {{role}} interview.
Practice each one with the STAR format: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

## 1. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate or manager. How did you handle it?

What interviewers look for: whether you can navigate conflict while keeping the working relationship intact.

Source: https://www.themuse.com/advice/behavioral-interview-questions-answers-examples

## 2. Describe the most challenging project you have worked on. What made it hard, and how did you get through it?

What interviewers look for: how you break down ambiguity, persist through setbacks, and measure the outcome.

Source: https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/interviewing/common-behavioral-interview-questions-and-how-to-answer-them

## 3. Tell me about a time you failed. What did you learn from it?

What interviewers look for: ownership of mistakes and evidence that you changed your approach afterward.

Source: https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/interviewing/how-to-answer-behavioral-interview-questions

## 4. Tell me about a time you had to influence someone over whom you had no authority.

What interviewers look for: persuasion through data and trust rather than title or hierarchy.

Source: https://www.amazon.jobs/principles

## 5. Describe a time you had to deliver important work under a tight deadline.

What interviewers look for: prioritization under pressure and how you communicate when trade-offs are needed.

Source: https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/interviewing/common-behavioral-interview-questions-and-how-to-answer-them

## 6. Tell me about a time you received critical feedback. How did you respond?

What interviewers look for: openness to feedback and concrete changes you made as a result.

Source: https://www.themuse.com/advice/behavioral-interview-questions-answers-examples

## 7. Give me an example of a challenging goal you set for yourself and how you achieved it.

What interviewers look for: ambition, planning, and follow-through when no one is watching.

Source: https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/interviewing/how-to-answer-behavioral-interview-questions

## 8. Tell me about a time you made a decision with incomplete information or took a calculated risk.

What interviewers look for: judgment under uncertainty and how you weighed the trade-offs before acting.

Source: https://www.amazon.jobs/principles
